- V89 | 2026-11-16 15:37 | Expanded the Lorentz-function help in both Chinese and English: the page now explicitly describes the route-similarity-weighted `kLor` multiplier (actual moving time divided by curve time) and the cross-validation shrinkage `k′Lor = 1 + 0.55 × (kLor − 1)` used before the pace correction is applied.
- V88 | 2026-11-16 15:30 | The pending Lorentz-fit preview now shows the same 0.55-shrunk route multiplier that is used after “Apply fit”, instead of showing the raw pre-shrinkage multiplier. This keeps the preview, status text and resulting route calculation consistent.
- V87 | 2026-11-16 14:54 | Lorentz route-level pace calibration now shrinks the similarity-weighted `kLor` toward 1 with a 0.55 factor selected by leave-one-out cross-validation on the v85 stable GPX subset. This reduces out-of-sample aggregate bias from the unshrunk fit while retaining the Lorentz curve's lower absolute error; the validation workflow and all-record sensitivity case are documented in the reusable maintenance skill.
- V86 | 2026-11-16 14:22 | GPX quality handling now uses distance-aware Hampel elevation cleaning before 20 m resampling, excludes records with at least 10 elevation jumps from automatic calibration, detects vehicle segments with 15 km/h entry and 8 km/h exit hysteresis plus duration/distance safeguards, reports trimmed vehicle distances and retained walking time, and splits long GPS gaps instead of treating them as walking or stops. Records that are too short after trimming or have gaps over five minutes are not used for automatic calibration.
